# Review choices: Hypothetical training-program study — proposed manuscript edits

Selecting an implementation option approves its included changes. Discuss Further and unselected issues are not approvals. Check all prerequisites, conflicting alternatives and edited numerical claims before applying wording.

## EX01: Four missing outcomes are being counted as known noncompletion

Choice: A. Report completion among students with observed outcomes

Comment:
Use the observed-outcome rate in Results and retain all 80 students in the recruitment count. Keep the four unknown outcomes visible.

### Participants — replace the complete-record claim under either option (participants-count)

Current passage:
Follow-up outcomes were observed for all 80 recruited students.

Proposed wording:
Follow-up outcomes were observed for 76 of the 80 recruited students.

### Results — completion among observed outcomes (results-observed)

Prerequisite: Option A: use the 76 observed outcomes as the denominator.

Current passage:
Forty-eight students completed the course (60.0%), while the remaining 32 did not complete it.

Proposed wording:
Among the 76 students with recorded outcomes, 48 completed the course (63.2%) and 28 did not (36.8%). Four of the 80 recruited students had no recorded follow-up outcome.
